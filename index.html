Constellation of the month

Orion, the
hunter overhead.

Visible worldwide, and one of the easiest patterns to learn — three stars in a row form the belt.

Betelgeuse, the reddish shoulder star, is a red supergiant nearing the end of its life — it could go supernova anytime in the next hundred thousand years.

Rigel, the blue-white foot, burns far hotter and is one of the brightest stars in the night sky.

The three belt stars — Alnitak, Alnilam, Mintaka — sit almost in a perfect line and are the fastest way to find Orion from anywhere in the sky.
